Office hours will be held in the GHC 5th floor Citadel Commons unless otherwise noted in the calendar below. TA office hours in the Citadel Commons will use [OHQueue](https://ohq.eberly.cmu.edu/#/courses), queue name "15-440".  Instructor office hours will not use an online queue.

**Please be warned:** Office hours will not be held in the 24 hour period before each project deadline.  Please start early!
